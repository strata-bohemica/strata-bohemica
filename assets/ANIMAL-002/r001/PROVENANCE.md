## R decisions (editable in `source/gen_pigs.py`)

- **Adult pig.**
  - Size: back about 0.77 m at the withers, snout to rump about 1.28 m.
  - Shape: long legs; a narrow, deep body that is higher at the shoulders; a long straight wedge head with a flat snout disc; small erect ears tipped forward; a dark bristle crest along the neck and back; a thin straight tail with a tassel.
  - Not modelled: tusks (the model is meant as a sow or barrow).
  - Coat colours: dark brown, red-brown, near-black and grizzled grey-brown, with bristle streak noise and a darker dorsal line.
- **Piglet.**
  - The adult scaled by 0.43, with a relatively larger head (×1.30), shorter snout (×0.78) and shorter body (×0.84).
  - An optional coat column with light longitudinal flank stripes, which is a wild-type trait. Whether Late Bronze Age domestic piglets were striped is not known.
- **Motion.**
  - Pig walk 1.0 m/s (lateral sequence, 0.5 m stride).
  - Piglet walk 0.5 m/s. Piglet trot 1.0 m/s (diagonal, short suspension) so it keeps up with the walking sow.
  - Rooting: 3 press-and-flick pushes per 3 s.
  - Lying: sternal lying, lowered front first and raised hind first. These sequences are inherited from ANIMAL-001.
- **Demo layout (F/R).**
  - Pigs under the oak, a sow with piglets, and pigs in a pen; one PERSON at the gate; two sheep.
  - The acorn litter, rooted-soil patches and the pen are preview-only primitives.
