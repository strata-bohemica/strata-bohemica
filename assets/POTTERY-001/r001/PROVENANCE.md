# POTTERY-001 r001 — provenance

Museum pottery package for SB-058 (Březnice 1.0, homestead hs-11): hand-forming of an unfired clay bowl, shown as an **experimental variant**. It holds one craft skin with three clips and the five MUSEUM-018 props. No firing, kiln, wheel or production claim is made.

## Owner decisions this package follows

Owner approval 2026-11-23 in conversation („schvaluji“), relayed by the dispatcher of SB-058:

1. **Licence.** Public release of the MUSEUM-018/019 models under **CC BY 4.0** is allowed. This package applies it to its models (see Licence below). No further conditions were set and none are added here.
2. **Choreography.** Solved by **splitting the clips** (arrival / cycle / leaving, no walking), not by changing the museum's CraftProps code.
3. **Scope.** The existing hand-forming of the MUSEUM-018 bowl as an experimental variant (BREZ-008, BREZ-010): no firing scene, no Hněvotín.

## Claim classes

As in `team/evidence/breznice/BREZ-008/README.md`, extended by the opus-v2 contract classes:

- **M** local evidence from Březnice u Bechyně, read in the cited source or data table;
- **A** analogy from another site, a general synthesis or an experiment;
- **O** own estimate or reading by an agent, not the authors' statement;
- **E** bounded source observation (opus-v2 COMMON.md);
- **R** reconstructed shape, scale, material or action, including project values (BREZ-008 class P);
- **F** fictional placement, people and display conventions.

## What each part rests on

| Part | Class | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Bowl type: rounded conical bowl (M2) | **M** | BREZ-008 §1a/§3: M2 is the commonest bowl subtype at Březnice, 46 of 133 typed bowls (ZEN DTB1, Kuna – Němcová – Chvojka 2022, doi 10.5281/zenodo.6475797). |
| Wall thickness of the bowls | **M** (range) / **R** (value) | BREZ-008 §3: M2 median wall 5 (probably mm), range 2–11 (M; unit O). MUSEUM-018 contact points 6.4 mm apart on the smoothed bowl, 11.5 mm on the raised one: inside the local range, value chosen (R). |
| Size 214 × 91 × 214 mm (smoothed), 178 × 64 × 178 mm (raised) | **R** | Project values of MUSEUM-018 (BREZ-008 class P). No Březnice measurement exists; the local profile with scale is in the unseen 2021 monograph (BREZ-008, owner closed the card without it). |
| Flat base | **not attested locally** / **A** | No M2 at Březnice has a recorded base (M, BREZ-008 §3). Flat bases on regional analogues Drhovice and Čížová (J08 Fig. 106: 3, 5) (A). Kept as R. |
| Straight rounded rim | **M** (straight) / **R** (rounding) | Rim O-1 „rovný“ on 38 of 46 M2 (M); lip rounding is a model choice. |
| Visible coils (`bowl_coil_raised`), coil building | **A** | General synthesis J08 p. 15 and experiments (AAS 14, 2022; EXARC 2021/3, cited by MUSEUM-018, not re-read here). Not documented on Březnice vessels (BREZ-008 part 2). |
| Finger smoothing of the wall | **M** (finishing) / **A** (as forming step) | Finger-wiped surfaces are recorded locally as finishing or decoration (V-231/V-232, BREZ-008 part 2), not as evidence of how the wall was built. |
| Work block, its height, clay stock and spare coil on it | **R** | MUSEUM-018 design choices; the block is a practical support hypothesis. |
| Crouching posture, both hands in/at the bowl, stroke arcs and rhythm | **R** | MUSEUM-019 authored performance (comparative and curatorial choice). |
| Clip split (arrival 3.45 s, cycle 9.1 s, leaving 3.45 s), standing in place at both ends | **F** | This package, owner decision (b). |
| Raised bowl through the whole cycle, one discrete swap to the smoothed bowl at the start of `form_exit` | **F** | Display convention (CraftProps can only show or hide a prop); manager decision after the PR #524 review. It does not say how long making a bowl took; the smoothing is not shown, and the finished bowl is not shown being set aside. |
| Earth-brown clay colours, bark/end grain | **R** | MUSEUM-018. |
| Unfired state; no firing | owner decision (c) | BREZ-010: firing scene deferred; kilns at Březnice are features with a burnt surface or fill, their use for pottery is not shown (M fact / O function). |

Laténský Hněvotín is not used as a shape or technique reference in this package.
