### Design decisions (R)

- **Oak-like tree.** A stout ~1.8 m trunk carries six crooked, spreading limbs and seven secondary branches, with restrained asymmetry.
  - Summer has 7 overlapping opaque lobes with outward cutout leaf cards.
  - Spring has small light-green cores plus two-sided sprigs, with no central lobe, so the crown is open and the branches stay readable.
  - Autumn has 4 sagging lobes in an ochre/russet mix. The leader and one limb are bare, a third limb keeps only sparse sprigs, and twig sprays fill the gaps.
  - Winter keeps the woody layout and adds two-sided twig-spray cards.
- **Hazel-like shrub.** 7 arching stems from a tight base. The leafed state adds 5 tall lobes and cards. The bare state uses upright twig cards, with no catkins.
- **Grass.** Generic dry clumps (straw/ochre, a few seed heads) and wet clumps (darker blue-green, upright), built from bent two-sided strips.
- Colours are restrained and earthy to match the existing village. There is no baked shadow, and lighting is left to the renderer.

### Deviations and notes

- **No doubleSided materials.** The contract would allow `doubleSided=true` on named foliage-card materials, but all cards use a single-sided MASK
  material. Where both sides must show, a separate reversed back face is emitted, so Godot lights each side with its own normal (no
  back-face normal flip). The extra triangles are counted in every budget.
- Leaf-card and lobe vertex normals are bent outward from the crown/lobe centres for soft volumetric shading.
- The oak crown cap of 3.8 m is held as a hard limit, without using the +10% tolerance (measured maximum 3.769 m).
- Every GLB embeds identical copies of the two atlases, which keeps each file self-contained. "Unique" textures means unique pixel content (2).
- No animations. The brief forbids built-in wind and season timers, so there are no loop checks to report.
- The ZIP verification result is kept outside the archive (see CHECKS) because it cannot describe itself.
