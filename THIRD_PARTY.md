# Third-party material

The SVG wordmark and tagline text in `docs/brand/` is converted to outlines from Fraunces (SemiBold), IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono, all published under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The font files are not included in this repository.

## Code in `site/vendor/`

- **three.js** r160, MIT (`site/vendor/three/LICENSE`), including the GLTFLoader, OrbitControls and SkeletonUtils examples.
- **meshopt decoder** from meshoptimizer 0.18, MIT, Copyright (C) 2016-2022 Arseny Kapoulkine (header in `site/vendor/three/examples/libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js`).

## Reference material

The models were built without copying third-party images or geometry. Reference photographs (some under CC BY-SA on Wikimedia Commons) were viewed only, as stated in each asset's `PROVENANCE.md`.
