# Research notes

Notes behind the info cards (`museum/data/crafts/*.json` cite them). Each claim is tagged E (evidenced at Březnice), R (regional analogy) or F (our reconstruction). The notes are working material, not peer-reviewed.

## What was checked against the primary sources (2026-11-19)

- Šálková et al. 2022 (Sci Rep 12: 20323, open access): 17% field species, ~13 ha, 100–150 years of occupation.
- Kováčik, Pavelka, Hořínková 2022 (Archeologické rozhledy 74/2): two Late Bronze Age ards from Opava, Silesia; first ard find in the Czech Republic.
- Not verified: items that cite sources that are paywalled or not online (e.g. Chvojka et al. 2019, 2021). Treat those as leads.

## How many homesteads?

The roughly 70 features at Březnice accumulate over 100–150 years (houses were burned and rebuilt each generation). The hinterland model of Šálková et al. 2022 supports 9 (optimal fields), 14 (extensive) or 20 (maximal, intensive) houses at the same time. The museum shows 12 at once, inside that range.
